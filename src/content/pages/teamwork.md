## Roles

<div class="goal"><b>Goal:</b> your team knows who does what in each lab and what you hand in.</div>
    <p>Teams have three or four students and at least two disciplines. Roles rotate each lab, so everyone uses the keyboard at least once in the no-code labs.</p>
    <div class="tw"><table>
      <tr><th>Role</th><th>Task in a lab</th><th>Usually</th></tr>
      <tr><td><b>Builder</b></td><td>Runs Remix or the no-code tool. Reads each step aloud before doing it.</td><td>CS in L-labs, anyone in G-labs</td></tr>
      <tr><td><b>Process analyst</b></td><td>Draws the state machine and tracks costs (gas, time, intermediaries).</td><td>BI</td></tr>
      <tr><td><b>Legal analyst</b></td><td>Writes the clause the code implements and lists what the code cannot express.</td><td>Law</td></tr>
      <tr><td><b>Recorder</b></td><td>Keeps the lab log: screenshots, addresses, transaction hashes, answers to Discuss questions.</td><td>Rotates</td></tr>
    </table></div>

## Deliverables

<h4>Lab log (each lab)</h4>
    <ul>
      <li>One page per lab: date, team, roles, the steps you completed, screenshots of key results.</li>
      <li>All addresses and transaction hashes. For Sepolia: Etherscan links.</li>
      <li>Short answers to the Discuss questions, one paragraph per discipline.</li>
      <li>One thing that went wrong and how you fixed it.</li>
    </ul>
    <h4>Portfolio (end of course)</h4>
    <ul>
      <li>A shared repository or folder with all lab logs and contracts.</li>
      <li>The capstone (P1): working contract, process model, legal text and a 10-minute presentation.</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="callout tip"><h5>How you are assessed</h5><p>The instructor notes (I3) contain the rubric: shared part for the team (does it work, do the three parts match?) and an individual part for each discipline.</p></div>
