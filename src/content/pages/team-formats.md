## Formats

<div class="card"><h4>1. Translate a contract</h4><ul>
      <li><b>Goal:</b> see the gap between legal text and code.</li>
      <li><b>Roles:</b> one student writes a rental clause, one models it as a process, one implements it, then swap; everyone tests it with the Tenderly simulator (G8).</li>
      <li><b>Deliverable:</b> clause, model, contract and a list of terms the code cannot express.</li>
    </ul></div>
    <div class="card"><h4>2. Audit and dispute</h4><ul>
      <li><b>Goal:</b> see the effect of a bug on all three areas.</li>
      <li><b>Roles:</b> one student finds the bug and runs the attack (L6), one calculates the loss, one argues liability and recovery; rotate in the next round.</li>
      <li><b>Deliverable:</b> an incident report with technical, economic and legal sections.</li>
    </ul></div>
    <div class="card"><h4>3. Board meeting on chain</h4><ul>
      <li><b>Goal:</b> experience corporate governance with a multisig.</li>
      <li><b>Roles:</b> three “directors” share a Safe (G3) or an Aragon DAO (G4); one proposes a payment that breaks the company policy; the others must notice and reject it.</li>
      <li><b>Deliverable:</b> minutes with Etherscan links and a payment policy.</li>
    </ul></div>
    <div class="card"><h4>4. Paper of the week</h4><ul>
      <li><b>Goal:</b> read current research through all three lenses.</li>
      <li><b>Roles:</b> one paper from F7; each student explains the method, the economic effect and the legal question in turn.</li>
      <li><b>Deliverable:</b> a 10-minute talk and a one-page summary.</li>
    </ul></div>
