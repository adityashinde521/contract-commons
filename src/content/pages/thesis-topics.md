## Topics

<ol>
      <li><b>LLM review versus classic tools:</b> run an AI assistant and Slither on the course labs (<a href="/lessons/lab-escrow/">4.3 Escrow</a>, <a href="/lessons/lab-rental-deposit/">4.4 Rental deposit</a>, <a href="/lessons/lab-reentrancy/">7.4 Reentrancy</a>). Measure true and false findings. (CS)</li>
      <li><b>Law to code with a DSL:</b> write one German tenancy clause in Stipula or Catala and in Solidity. Compare what each can express. (Law + CS)</li>
      <li><b>Cost of trust:</b> compare the full cost of Escrow on L1, on an L2, and with a bank escrow service. (BI)</li>
      <li><b>Agent accountability:</b> map ERC-8004 identity and reputation to legal concepts of agency and attribution. (Law + CS)</li>
      <li><b>Attestations as evidence:</b> how should German courts weigh EAS attestations compared with qualified electronic signatures? (Law)</li>
      <li><b>Crypto-agility pattern:</b> design a contract where the signature check can be replaced, and analyse the governance risk this creates. (CS + Law)</li>
      <li><b>MEV and fairness:</b> explain sandwich attacks to non-experts and assess them under consumer-protection and market-abuse rules. (Law + BI)</li>
      <li><b>GUI tools in legal education:</b> test <a href="/lessons/lab-safe/">11.3 Safe</a>, <a href="/lessons/lab-aragon/">11.4 Aragon DAO</a> and <a href="/lessons/lab-tenderly/">8.3 Tenderly</a> with students without a programming background and measure which concepts they understand afterwards. (BI + education)</li>
      <li><b>Tool evaluation:</b> evaluate a new visual builder from <a href="/lessons/tools-to-evaluate/">13.4 Tools to evaluate</a> with the checklist and compare it with <a href="/lessons/lab-openzeppelin-wizard/">5.3 OpenZeppelin Wizard</a> and <a href="/lessons/lab-thirdweb/">5.4 thirdweb</a>. (CS + BI)</li>
    </ol>
