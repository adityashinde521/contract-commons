## Rubric

<p>Use for the capstone and, in short form, for lab logs. Level 3 is the pass standard.</p>
    <div class="tw"><table>
      <tr><th>Criterion</th><th>2 Basic</th><th>3 Good</th><th>4 Excellent</th></tr>
      <tr><td>Concepts</td><td>Defines terms.</td><td>Explains how hashes, signatures and consensus give a tamper-evident ledger.</td><td>Also explains trade-offs: finality, cost, privacy.</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Building (code or no-code)</td><td>Runs given steps.</td><td>Changes a rule and predicts the effect correctly.</td><td>Designs a new rule and tests failure cases.</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Security</td><td>Names attacks.</td><td>Shows an attack and its fix.</td><td>Also covers keys, signing and operations.</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Three languages</td><td>One discipline only.</td><td>Explains the contract for CS, BI and law.</td><td>Uses legal and business cases to justify design choices.</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Legal analysis</td><td>Lists issues.</td><td>Applies a concrete rule (for example §551 BGB, GDPR, MiCA) to the contract.</td><td>Proposes a clause or code change that solves the issue.</td></tr>
      <tr><td>Documentation</td><td>Incomplete log.</td><td>Complete log with links and screenshots.</td><td>Another team could repeat the work from it.</td></tr>
    </table></div>
